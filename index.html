🏐 A Beginner's Guide to Volleyball



ཻུ۪۪♡. Basic Rules
  • Each team can touch the ball a maximum of three times before it must be sent over the net
  • Service must be hit before the line
  • A player cannot hit the ball twice in a row unless it is a block
  • No net touch
ཻུ۪۪♡. Basic Techniques
  • Passing - Using your forearms to direct the ball to a teammate
  • Setting - Using your fingertips to push the ball to a teammate for an attack
  • Hitting/Spiking - Jumping and hitting the ball with force over the net
  • Blocking - Jumping at the net to prevent the ball from coming over
ཻུ۪۪♡. Volleyball Equipment
  • Volleyball - The ball used in the game
  • Knee Pads - Protective gear worn on the knees to prevent injuries during dives and falls
  • Volleyball Shoes - Shoes made for indoor or outdoor play that provides grip and support.
